How to play ?

Objective: Reach the final square (usually 100) first to win the game.

Starting the Game: All players start on square 1 and take turns rolling a virtual dice.

Movement: Move forward by the number rolled; climb ladders if you land on them and slide down snakes.

Winning: You must land exactly on the final square to win.

Rolling a number higher will make you move back.

Bonus Rule: Rolling a 6 gives an extra turn.
